# must reach in the long tier (long.sh), one message per line. A property here
# that a long run never reaches fails it (a MISS on the floor); a line that
# names no property fails it too, as a floor gone stale.

# and rough, reach them all, 2026-10-05) and all but one of fat16.zig's and
